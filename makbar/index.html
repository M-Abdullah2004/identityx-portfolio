About Me

With over 27 years of engineering excellence in Utilities Management, I provide strategic oversight across project planning, feasibility studies, design reviews, technical evaluations, utility cost optimization, installation supervision, and O&M governance.

I hold a B.E. (Mechanical Engineering) degree from NED University of Engineering & Technology, Karachi (Batch 1993–94), and have enhanced my Business Communication skills through a professional course at the IBA-City Campus, Karachi — graduating with First Position.

I co-founded Enerzone Engineering Services (EES) in 2008, serving initially as Manager Projects, then transitioning to Director full-time in 2009. EES is a PEC-registered consultancy based at S.I.T.E., Karachi, delivering multidisciplinary engineering solutions for industrial and commercial-scale facilities.

Currently active in Energy Audits & Compliance under NEECA and Pakistan-Accord, supporting industries to transition toward sustainable, safe, and energy-efficient operations.
